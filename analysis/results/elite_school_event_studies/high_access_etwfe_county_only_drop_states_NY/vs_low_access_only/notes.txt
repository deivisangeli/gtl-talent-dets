=== High access vs low-access counties only ===

States dropped from the panel: NY

Treated:  high-access counties (g = first fully exposed decade).
Control:  low-access counties (g = 0).
          Counties with no elite school are excluded from this spec.

Panel decades: 1800 to 2000
School age assumption: 14
School file: ../prep/output/elite_high_schools_core_1800_1930.csv

Sample restriction (applies to BOTH estimators)
- Counties with fewer than 4 non-missing pre-treatment outcome
  observations are dropped before any regression. The trend
  specification needs at least 4 pre-period cells to identify a
  county-specific slope with 2 residual degrees of freedom; we apply
  the same sample restriction to the plain estimator so the two lines
  in each figure are directly comparable.

Estimators
- Wooldridge ETWFE:
    Implemented as fixest::sunab(g, decade, ref.p = -10) with county
    and decade fixed effects. Sun and Abraham's saturated cohort x
    event-time regression is the same model as Wooldridge's ETWFE
    with anti-treatment leads. The reference event time is fixed at
    e = -10 (one decade before the school opens), so ATT(e = -10) =
    0 by construction. e = 0 is the partial-exposure decade (school
    opens this decade; cohorts born early in the decade reach age 14
    just as the school opens, so they receive partial exposure).
    e = +10 is the first fully-exposed decade. e = -20, -30, ... are
    honest pre-period placebo estimates of the pre-trend.
    Standard errors are clustered at the county level.

- Wooldridge ETWFE + county pre-period trend:
    Step 1 fits y_it = a_i + b_i * decade by OLS for each county on
    pre-treatment cells only (decade < g for treated counties; all
    decades for control counties with g = 0).
    Step 2 subtracts the fitted path from y to obtain y_resid.
    Step 3 feeds y_resid into the same sunab regression as the plain
    estimator above.
    Caveat: standard errors do not propagate first-step uncertainty.
