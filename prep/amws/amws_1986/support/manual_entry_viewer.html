How to read this comparison

Word fragments retain their original paragraph numbers and are shown separately. Generated list labels are identified explicitly. Raw Paddle blocks retain their original labels and boundaries; a block can contain multiple biographies. Use the full-document links to search for displaced text.

A line surviving in another paragraph is a structure defect, not a recognition omission. Error examples illustrate the judgment; they are not an exhaustive transcription or a CER/WER calculation. “Uncertain” can mean different strengths in the two methods, rather than uncertain identity alignment.

Small stratified sample. Results describe the reviewed entries, not the entire corpus. Prior deliveries and production data were preserved. Paragraph-repaired PaddleOCR is outside this comparison.
